# DimmingLab: Real-User Usability Pilot Protocol

This document defines the usability testing protocol and anonymous results recording template for high-school students, astronomy club members, and science educators.

---

## 1. Usability Test Protocol

### Target Participants:
- 1 Science Teacher / Astronomy Club Advisor
- 6–10 High-School Learners (Physics / Earth & Space Science students)

### Environment:
- Standard classroom laptops / Chromebooks / tablets.
- Browser: Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox.
- Mode: Tested online first, followed by offline disconnect test.

### Participant Tasks (Unguided):
1. **Task 1 (Discovery):** Open DimmingLab from the browser. Select "Kepler-8 b: The Repeating Transit".
2. **Task 2 (Alignment):** Adjust the trial period slider or launch the "Search for Repeating Dips" tool to align the transit dip at phase 0.0.
3. **Task 3 (Scientific Challenge):** Open "The Convincing Impostor" (Case B). Examine the Evidence Rail. Identify why folding at half-period is deceptive.
4. **Task 4 (Reasoning & Export):** Write a one-paragraph conclusion with an alternative explanation. Generate and print/download the Evidence Report.

## 3. Post-Activity Reflection Prompts
1. "When you adjusted the period slider, did you understand what the graph was showing?"
2. "Was it clear why alternating depths indicate a binary star rather than a planet?"
3. "Did the evidence report accurately capture your scientific reasoning?"
4. "What was confusing or frustrating about the controls?"
